FAQ

Returns Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about returns.

How long do I have to return an item?
You have up to 30 days from the date of delivery to initiate a return. Items must be in their original condition.
Is return shipping free?
Yes! We provide free return shipping labels for all returns initiated through our portal.
How long does it take to get my refund?
Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are typically processed within 5-7 business days.
Can I return an item without a tracking number?
We strongly recommend using the tracking number to initiate the return. If you don't have it, please contact our customer service team for assistance.
