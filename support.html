FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common support queries.

How do I track my shipment?

Use our Tracking page and enter your tracking number (e.g., VX123456).

What are your shipping rates?

Rates depend on package size, weight, and destination. Get a free quote on our Contact page.

How do I file a claim for a lost parcel?

Submit a ticket below or email claims@velloxlogistics.com with your tracking number and details.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes – we serve over 220 countries. Visit our Freight page for more.

How do I update my address?

Log into your account dashboard (if enabled) or contact our support team for assistance.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for business accounts.
