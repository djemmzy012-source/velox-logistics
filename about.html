WHO WE ARE

Built on Trust & Excellence

Founded in 2005, Velox Logistics has grown from a small courier service into a global logistics powerhouse. We serve over 220 countries and territories with a focus on speed, reliability, and customer experience.

Our network spans air, ocean, road, and rail — giving our customers the flexibility to ship anything from documents to heavy cargo with complete visibility and control. We invest heavily in technology to provide real-time tracking, automated customs clearance, and proactive notifications.
