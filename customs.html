How It Works

Our Customs Process

From documentation to clearance, we handle every step of the customs process with precision and care.

1

Documentation

We prepare and review all required customs documentation, including commercial invoices, packing lists, and certificates of origin.

2

Classification

Our experts classify your goods using Harmonized System (HS) codes to determine applicable duties, taxes, and trade restrictions.

3

Declaration

We file accurate customs declarations and manage all communications with customs authorities to ensure smooth processing.

4

Clearance

Your goods clear customs efficiently, and we provide real-time updates on status, duties paid, and estimated delivery timing.

Essential Documents We Handle

We manage all the critical paperwork required for smooth customs clearance across borders.

Commercial Invoice

Detailed invoice showing the value, quantity, and description of goods for customs valuation purposes.

Packing List

Comprehensive list of all items in the shipment with weights, dimensions, and packaging details.

Certificate of Origin

Official document certifying the country where the goods were manufactured or produced.

HS Code Classification

Harmonized System codes that accurately categorize your goods for duty and tax calculation.

Bill of Lading / Air Waybill

Shipping documents that serve as evidence of the contract of carriage and receipt of goods.

Free Trade Agreement Forms

Documentation to claim preferential duty rates under existing free trade agreements.
